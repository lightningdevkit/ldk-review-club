> WHAT IS THIS?
A fortnightly club for reviewing LDK PRs, together, in public.
> WHAT'S IT FOR?
Helping newer contributors learn the LDK review process. It's not primarily intended to help open PRs get merged.
> WHO SHOULD TAKE PART?
Anyone who wants to learn about contributing to LDK. All are welcome to come and ask questions!
> WHO RUNS THIS?
Meetings are scheduled by {{ site.coordinator }}. The meetings are hosted by a variety of LDK contributors.
