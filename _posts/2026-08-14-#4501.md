## Notes
[Blinded Payment Path](https://github.com/lightning/bolts/blob/master/proposals/route-blinding.md) is a technique that provides anonymity for the recipient of a payment. The process requires the recipient node to chose an introduction point/node, and construct a blinded route from the introduction point/node to themself. Then hand it over to the sender who constructs a route from themself to the introduction point, extends the route with the recipient's blinded route, and creates an onion for the whole route.

However, when the introduction point is close to the recipient, observers or the sender can detect the recipient node. So the recipient is allowed to add dummy hops to the blinded route, that way observers will not be able to distinguish between the dummy hops and the blinded hops.

This PR builds on the work that introduced Dummy Hops to blinded payment path by adding realistic relay parameters that ensures observers can't distinguish between the dummy hops and the blinded hops.
